What does “Night: Coverage Gap” mean?

If the minimum is one L1 analyst for the Night shift, but the current schedule assigns zero L1 analysts to Night, ShiftGuard flags a coverage gap. It does not mean your actual SOC is understaffed. This demo has no access to your real roster or HR systems.

For real operations, review approved leave, on-call arrangements, shift handovers, rest periods, skills, and local employment requirements. The CSV is an editable planning export, not a validated compliance record.
